SECTION 1: REMOTE WORK POLICY
Full-time employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.
Eligible remote employees receive an annual home office stipend of USD 500 per year.

SECTION 2: EXPENSE CLAIMS & TRAVEL
Travel meals are reimbursed up to USD 60 per day with itemized receipts.
Expense receipts must be submitted within 14 days of trip completion.
Airfare and hotel bookings over USD 1,000 require pre-approval from the department head.
