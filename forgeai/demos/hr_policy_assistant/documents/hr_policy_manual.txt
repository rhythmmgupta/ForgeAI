SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICIES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1 Annual Leave (Vacation)
- All full-time shore staff are entitled to 21 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Leave accrues at 1.75 days per month of continuous service.
- Annual leave must be approved by the department head at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried forward to the next calendar year.
- Leave exceeding 5 consecutive days requires HR notification.
- Probationary employees (first 6 months) may take up to 5 days annual leave.

1.2 Sick Leave
- Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
- Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
- Unused sick leave does NOT carry forward and cannot be encashed.
- Employees must notify their supervisor within 2 hours of their shift start.
- Extended medical leave beyond 12 days may be considered unpaid or under FMLA equivalent.

1.3 Maternity & Paternity Leave
- Female employees: 26 weeks of paid maternity leave (first two children). 12 weeks for
  subsequent children.
- Male employees: 15 days of paid paternity leave within 6 months of child's birth.
- Adoption leave: 12 weeks from date of legal adoption for children under 3 years.
- All parental leave requires 8 weeks advance notice when possible.

1.4 Bereavement Leave
- 5 days paid leave for death of immediate family (spouse, parent, child, sibling).
- 3 days paid leave for extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt, uncle).
- Additional unpaid leave may be granted at manager's discretion.

1.5 Public Holidays
- OceanWave observes 12 paid public holidays per year.
- Employees required to work on holidays receive 200% of normal pay (double time).
- Floating holiday: each employee gets 1 additional floating holiday per year.

SECTION 2: EMPLOYEE BENEFITS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1 Health Insurance
- Company provides group health insurance covering employee, spouse, and up to 2 dependents.
- Coverage includes hospitalization, outpatient treatment, dental, and vision.
- Annual coverage limit: INR 5,00,000 (approximately USD 60,000) per family.
- Employees may upgrade to premium plan by paying the differential (approximately INR 5,000/month).
- Pre-existing conditions are covered after 2 years of continuous employment.

2.2 Retirement Benefits
- Provident Fund (PF): Company contributes 12% of basic salary. Employee contributes 12%.
- Gratuity: Payable after 5 years of continuous service. Calculated as:
  15 days wages × years of service ÷ 26.
- Superannuation: Optional. Company contributes 5% of basic salary for employees enrolled.

2.3 Education Assistance
- Reimbursement up to INR 50,000 per year for job-relevant courses and certifications.
- Requires pre-approval from department head and HR.
- Employee must maintain a "B" grade or equivalent.
- Reimbursement processed within 30 days of submitting completion certificate.

2.4 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Free confidential counseling for employees and family members.
- Covers mental health, financial planning, legal consultation, and substance abuse.
- Available 24/7 via phone hotline: 1800-555-0199.
- Up to 8 sessions per year at no cost.

2.5 Transportation
- Shuttle service from designated pickup points for shifts starting before 7 AM or ending after 9 PM.
- Fuel reimbursement: INR 6 per km for employees using personal vehicles (requires log).
- Parking provided at all office locations at no cost.

SECTION 3: CODE OF CONDUCT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1 Professional Standards
- Employees must maintain professional behavior at all times during work hours.
- Dress code: Business casual Monday-Thursday. Casual Friday permitted.
- Personal phone use should be limited to break times. Emergency calls are always permitted.
- Company equipment must not be used for personal purposes.

3.2 Anti-Harassment Policy
- OceanWave maintains zero-tolerance for harassment of any kind.
- Harassment includes: sexual harassment, bullying, intimidation, discrimination based on
  race, gender, religion, age, disability, or sexual orientation.
- All complaints are investigated within 7 business days.
- Retaliation against complainants is strictly prohibited and grounds for termination.

3.3 Confidentiality
- Employees must not disclose proprietary information, trade secrets, or client data.
- NDA must be signed upon joining and renewed annually.
- Violation of confidentiality may result in immediate termination and legal action.
- Social media policy: employees may not post about company operations or clients.

3.4 Substance Abuse
- Use of illegal substances on company premises is prohibited.
- Alcohol consumption is not permitted during work hours or on company premises.
- Random drug testing may be conducted. Positive results lead to immediate suspension
  pending investigation.
- Company supports rehabilitation: employees may access EAP for assistance.

3.5 Conflict of Interest
- Employees must disclose any potential conflict of interest to their supervisor.
- Outside employment requires prior written approval from HR.
- Employees may not hold financial interest in a competitor or supplier.

SECTION 4: GRIEVANCE PROCEDURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1 Informal Resolution
- Employees are encouraged to first discuss concerns directly with their supervisor.
- HR Business Partners are available for guidance on informal resolution.
- Target resolution time: 5 business days.

4.2 Formal Grievance Process
- Step 1: Submit written grievance to HR via email or grievance portal.
- Step 2: HR acknowledges receipt within 2 business days.
- Step 3: Investigation initiated within 5 business days.
- Step 4: Investigation findings and resolution proposed within 15 business days.
- Step 5: If unsatisfied, employee may appeal to the Grievance Committee within 10 days.

4.3 Grievance Committee
- Composed of: HR Director, one department head (not from complainant's department),
  and one employee representative.
- Committee convenes within 10 business days of appeal.
- Decision is final and binding.
- Anonymous grievances are accepted but may limit investigation scope.

4.4 Whistleblower Protection
- Employees reporting illegal or unethical behavior are protected from retaliation.
- Anonymous reporting available via ethics hotline: 1800-555-0200.
- Investigation conducted by external party for impartiality.

SECTION 5: WORKPLACE SAFETY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

5.1 General Safety
- All employees must complete safety orientation within first week of employment.
- Fire drills conducted quarterly. Participation is mandatory.
- First aid kits located at all floor exits and in break rooms.
- Incident reporting: all accidents must be reported within 24 hours.

5.2 Emergency Procedures
- In case of fire: evacuate via nearest exit, assemble at designated muster points.
- Earthquake: drop, cover, hold. Evacuate only after shaking stops.
- Medical emergency: call internal emergency number 555 and notify security.
- Building evacuation officers identified by orange vests.

5.3 Ergonomics
- Ergonomic assessments available upon request for all workstations.
- Standing desks available on request (approval from facilities team).
- Mandatory 10-minute break for every 2 hours of continuous screen work.

5.4 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Required PPE for warehouse and dock staff: hard hat, safety shoes, high-visibility vest.
- Safety goggles required when operating cutting or grinding equipment.
- PPE provided at company expense. Replacement PPE issued upon return of worn items.
