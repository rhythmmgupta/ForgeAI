OCEANWAVE MARITIME SERVICES — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK SUPPLEMENT
===========================================================

Effective Date: March 2024
Department: Human Resources

SECTION A: WORK SCHEDULES & ATTENDANCE
═══════════════════════════════════════

A.1 Standard Work Hours
- Shore staff: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (1 hour lunch break).
- Warehouse staff: Rotating shifts — 6 AM to 2 PM, 2 PM to 10 PM, 10 PM to 6 AM.
- Weekend work requires pre-approval and attracts 150% pay (time-and-a-half).
- Flexible working hours available for Grade C and above with manager approval.

A.2 Remote Work Policy
- Eligible positions may work remotely up to 2 days per week.
- Remote work requires a stable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps).
- Employees must be reachable during core hours (10 AM to 4 PM).
- Remote work agreement must be signed and renewed every 6 months.
- Equipment for remote work: company provides laptop and headset.

A.3 Attendance & Punctuality
- Three instances of unexcused tardiness (>15 minutes late) in a month triggers
  a verbal warning.
- Habitual absenteeism (5+ unexcused absences in a quarter) may result in
  written warning followed by suspension.
- Time tracking: all employees must badge in/out. Remote employees log via
  the HR portal.

SECTION B: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
══════════════════════════════════

B.1 Performance Review Cycle
- Annual performance reviews conducted in December.
- Mid-year check-in in June (informal, developmental).
- 360-degree feedback available for Grade C and above.
- Performance ratings: Exceptional (5), Exceeds Expectations (4),
  Meets Expectations (3), Needs Improvement (2), Unsatisfactory (1).

B.2 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
- Triggered when employee receives a rating of 2 or below.
- PIP duration: 90 days with bi-weekly check-ins.
- Failure to improve during PIP may result in termination.
- Employees on PIP are not eligible for promotions or transfers.

B.3 Promotions & Transfers
- Eligibility: minimum 18 months in current role.
- Internal job postings available on the company intranet.
- Transfer between departments requires both managers' approval.
- Promotion decisions consider performance, competency, and business need.

SECTION C: COMPENSATION
═══════════════════════

C.1 Pay Structure
- Salaries are reviewed annually during the compensation cycle (March).
- Salary ranges are benchmarked against maritime industry standards.
- Pay grades: A (Entry), B (Experienced), C (Senior), D (Manager), E (Director).
- Overtime: eligible employees (Grade A & B) receive 150% for overtime hours.

C.2 Bonuses
- Annual performance bonus: 0-20% of annual salary based on individual rating
  and company performance.
- Spot awards: up to INR 25,000 for exceptional contributions (manager-nominated).
- Referral bonus: INR 15,000 for successful employee referrals (paid after 6 months
  of referee's employment).

C.3 Salary Advance
- Employees may request a salary advance of up to 1 month's gross salary.
- Maximum 2 advances per financial year.
- Advance is recovered in 3 equal monthly installments.
- Request must be submitted via HR portal with justification.

SECTION D: SEPARATION & OFFBOARDING
════════════════════════════════════

D.1 Resignation
- Notice period: 30 days for Grade A & B, 60 days for Grade C and above.
- Notice period buyout available at the discretion of management.
- Exit interview conducted by HR on the last working day.
- Full and final settlement within 45 days of last working day.

D.2 Termination
- Grounds for termination: performance issues (after PIP failure), misconduct,
  redundancy, or business restructuring.
- Immediate termination for gross misconduct (theft, fraud, violence, harassment).
- Severance pay: 1 month salary per completed year of service (for redundancy only).
- Company property must be returned on the last working day.

D.3 Retirement
- Standard retirement age: 60 years.
- Early retirement option available from age 55 with mutual agreement.
- Retirement benefits: gratuity + provident fund + any superannuation benefits.
- Post-retirement health insurance: company covers 50% of premium for 2 years
  post-retirement.
