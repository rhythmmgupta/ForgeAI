OCEANWAVE MARITIME SERVICES — PROCUREMENT GUIDELINES
=======================================================

Document: PROC-2024-001
Effective: February 2024
Classification: Internal

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 1: PROCUREMENT PROCESS OVERVIEW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1 Purpose
This document establishes the procurement procedures for ship spare parts,
marine equipment, and vessel maintenance supplies for OceanWave Maritime Services.
All procurement personnel must follow these guidelines to ensure cost-effective,
timely, and compliant purchasing.

1.2 Procurement Authority Levels
- Up to USD 1,000: Approved by Department Supervisor
- USD 1,001 to USD 10,000: Approved by Procurement Manager
- USD 10,001 to USD 50,000: Approved by Head of Procurement
- USD 50,001 to USD 200,000: Approved by CFO
- Above USD 200,000: Board approval required

1.3 Vendor Selection Criteria
- Certification: Vendor must hold relevant ISO/maritime certifications
- Delivery reliability: >95% on-time delivery rate required
- Quality record: Less than 2% defect rate over past 12 months
- Pricing: Must be within 15% of market benchmark
- Payment terms: Net 30 minimum
- Geographic coverage: Ability to deliver to major ports

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 2: ORDERING PROCEDURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1 Standard Orders
- All orders must be placed through the ERP system (SAP S/4HANA).
- Purchase requisition (PR) must be created before purchase order (PO).
- PR requires budget validation and category manager approval.
- PO must reference the vessel or project number.
- Three-way matching required: PO, Goods Receipt, Invoice.

2.2 Emergency Orders
- Emergency orders permitted when vessel safety or operations are at risk.
- Emergency PO can be verbal but must be followed by written PO within 24 hours.
- Emergency orders bypass standard approval for amounts up to USD 25,000.
- Emergency order premium: vendors may charge up to 20% over standard pricing.
- All emergency orders must be documented with justification in the incident log.

2.3 Blanket Orders
- For recurring items (filters, gaskets, consumables), blanket orders are preferred.
- Blanket order period: 12 months maximum.
- Quantity variations: up to ±20% from estimated annual quantity.
- Pricing is locked for the blanket order period.
- Quarterly review of blanket order utilization required.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 3: INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1 Stock Levels
- Critical spares (safety equipment, engine parts): maintain minimum 3-month stock.
- Standard spares: maintain minimum 1-month stock based on average consumption.
- Consumables: reorder when stock falls below 2-week supply.
- Obsolete inventory review: conducted annually in January.

3.2 Warehouse Operations
- All received goods must be inspected within 48 hours.
- Inspection checklist includes: quantity verification, visual quality check,
  certification document review, shelf life verification.
- Non-conforming goods must be quarantined and vendor notified within 72 hours.
- Storage conditions: spares requiring controlled environment stored in climate-controlled
  zones (15-25°C, <60% humidity).

3.3 Inventory Valuation
- Method: Weighted Average Cost (WAC).
- Physical inventory count: bi-annually (June and December).
- Variance tolerance: 2% by value, 5% by quantity.
- Write-off authority: same as procurement authority levels.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 4: SUPPLIER MANAGEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1 Approved Supplier List (ASL)
- Only ASL vendors may supply critical safety and engine parts.
- ASL review conducted annually.
- New vendor onboarding: requires quality audit, financial assessment,
  and reference checks (minimum 3 references).
- Vendor onboarding time: typically 4-6 weeks.

4.2 Supplier Performance Metrics
- On-time delivery rate (target: >95%)
- Quality acceptance rate (target: >98%)
- Responsiveness (quote turnaround <48 hours)
- Price competitiveness (within 10% of best quote)
- Documentation accuracy (invoices, certificates)

4.3 Supplier Development
- Annual supplier conference for top 20 suppliers.
- Joint improvement programs for underperforming suppliers.
- Preferred supplier status: awarded based on consistent A-grade performance
  over 24 months. Benefits include early payment (Net 15) and volume guarantees.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 5: COMPLIANCE & DOCUMENTATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

5.1 Regulatory Requirements
- All spare parts must comply with classification society rules (ABS, DNV GL, Lloyd's).
- Safety equipment must hold valid SOLAS certification.
- Environmental equipment must comply with MARPOL regulations.
- Hazardous materials must include MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet).

5.2 Record Retention
- Purchase orders: retained for 7 years.
- Inspection records: retained for vessel's operational life.
- Supplier contracts: retained for contract period + 5 years.
- Certificates of conformity: retained for equipment operational life.

5.3 Audit
- Internal procurement audit: annually by Internal Audit department.
- External audit: as part of annual ISO 9001 certification.
- Port State Control inspections may review spare parts documentation.
- All audit findings must be remediated within 60 days.
